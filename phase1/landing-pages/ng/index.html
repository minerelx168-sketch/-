What is NCC-DMS and what changed in October 2025?

The Nigerian Communications Commission (NCC) launched the Device Management System (NCC-DMS) — a Central Equipment Identity Register that all MNOs (MTN, Glo, Airtel, 9mobile) must connect to. The system blocks stolen, cloned, or unapproved IMEIs from the network. Enforcement is rolling out gradually through 2026.

4-step NCC-DMS registration flow

  1. Find your IMEI — dial *#06# to see your 15-digit IMEI.
  2. Verify with imeihub — paste it in the form above to confirm brand, model and any flags.
  3. Register with your MNO — your carrier will collect IMEI data and forward to NCC-DMS automatically when you activate a SIM.
  4. Keep proof of purchase — for any disputes, NCC-DMS resolution requires invoice + ID.

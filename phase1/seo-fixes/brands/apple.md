# Apple IMEI Check — Free Lookup for Apple Devices

Apple's iPhone commands roughly a quarter of the global smartphone market and outright dominates in the United States, Japan, and much of Western Europe. From the entry-level iPhone SE to the titanium-framed iPhone 16 Pro Max, every Apple device ships with a unique 15-digit IMEI (International Mobile Equipment Identity) baked into its cellular hardware — the digital fingerprint that carriers, insurance companies, and second-hand marketplaces rely on to verify authenticity.

If you're buying a used iPhone, filing an insurance claim, or verifying that a listing is genuine before you pay, running an Apple IMEI check is the single fastest step you can take. Apple's ecosystem adds complexity most Android brands don't have: iCloud Activation Lock can render a device unusable if the previous owner didn't sign out, and Mobile Device Management (MDM) profiles from schools or employers can silently restrict features long after resale.

Our free IMEI lookup identifies your Apple model and specs instantly, and our low-cost premium checks reveal exactly what you need to know before money changes hands — starting at $0.01 for an iCloud status check.

## How to Find Your Apple IMEI

There are four reliable ways to locate the IMEI on an Apple device:

- **Dial `*#06#`** on the Phone app — the IMEI appears on-screen. Works on every iPhone in every region.
- **Settings → General → About** — scroll down to find IMEI, MEID, and ICCID for both physical and eSIM lines.
- **Original retail packaging** — the box has a barcode label listing the IMEI, model number, and serial.
- **SIM tray** — on iPhone 6 through iPhone 13, the IMEI is laser-etched on the metal SIM tray. On US eSIM-only models (iPhone 14 and later) the number is engraved inside the SIM slot cavity or omitted entirely.

For iPads with cellular or Apple Watches with LTE, the IMEI lives in **Settings → General → About** or on the rear engraving.

## When Should You Run an Apple IMEI Check?

Run an IMEI check on any Apple device in these scenarios:

- **Buying used on eBay, Facebook Marketplace, or a local classifieds site** — verify the model matches the listing, then run the $0.01 iCloud ON/OFF to make sure the seller has signed out of Find My. An iPhone locked to someone else's iCloud is essentially worthless.
- **Insurance claim or theft report** — carriers require the IMEI to blocklist a stolen device on the GSMA registry.
- **Warranty verification** — pull the AppleCare warranty status before paying a repair shop, or before buying from a reseller who claims "still under warranty."
- **Importing from another country** — an iPhone bought in Japan may be shutter-sound-locked; a US carrier-locked unit won't work on your home network without unlocking. The SIM-Lock and Carrier reports flag both.
- **Enterprise or school-issued devices** — the MDM check reveals hidden management profiles that could brick the phone after resale.
- **Suspected counterfeit** — Apple's IMEI database is authoritative; a fake iPhone will either return no result or a mismatched model.

## What to Do If Your Apple IMEI Check Reveals a Problem

If iCloud ON returns positive on a device you've just bought, contact the seller immediately and ask them to sign out of Find My iPhone remotely from iCloud.com — this is free and takes about a minute. If they can't or won't, request a refund on the spot. Do not attempt to bypass Activation Lock through unofficial channels; the only legitimate path is proof-of-purchase escalation to Apple support, and even then Apple's approval rate is low.

For a Lost or Stolen result, treat the transaction as void — the device is effectively unusable on any network worldwide, and Apple will not remove the flag without the original owner's cooperation. Report the encounter through your local marketplace's fraud channel so the seller's account is flagged.

If the MDM check reveals an active management profile, ask the previous owner to release the device from their MDM console (Jamf, Microsoft Intune, Kandji, Cisco Meraki). Without release, the profile reasserts itself after every factory reset, silently reapplying enterprise restrictions.

**Q: Can I check Apple iCloud status for free?**

**Q: What's the difference between iCloud ON/OFF and Clean/Lost?**
A: ON/OFF ($0.01) tells you whether Find My / Activation Lock is currently enabled. Clean/Lost ($0.03) tells you whether the device has been reported lost or stolen in Apple's database — a lost-marked iPhone will not activate even if you can bypass the previous owner.

**Q: Does an IMEI check work on eSIM-only iPhones sold in the US?**
A: Yes. eSIM iPhones still have an IMEI (in fact, they usually have two — one per line). Dial `*#06#` or check Settings → General → About.

**Q: My iPhone was engraved with a name — will that show up?**
A: No. Engravings are cosmetic and don't appear in Apple's IMEI database. They also don't affect resale value from an authentication standpoint.

**Q: Can imeihub unlock or bypass iCloud?**

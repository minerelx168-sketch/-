# Huawei IMEI Check — Free Lookup for Huawei Devices

Huawei is the third-largest smartphone maker in China and a top-five global brand across Asia, the Middle East, Africa, and Latin America. After the 2019 US Entity List restrictions cut off Google Mobile Services, Huawei rebuilt its entire software stack around HarmonyOS and its own AppGallery — and by 2024, with the Kirin 9000S silicon inside the Mate 60 series and now Mate 70, the brand had staged one of the most watched comebacks in consumer electronics. Every Huawei phone, tablet, and cellular wearable ships with a unique 15-digit IMEI (dual IMEIs on most 4G/5G models) that identifies the device on every carrier network in the world.

Huawei's second-hand market is one of the largest and most complicated of any smartphone brand. Cross-border resale between China, Hong Kong, Southeast Asia, and the Middle East is enormous, regional software builds differ dramatically (EMUI vs HarmonyOS, Google Services present or absent), and counterfeit Huawei devices are a well-documented problem — particularly around P and Mate series flagships. A pre-purchase IMEI check is the single fastest sanity test for authenticity.

Our free Huawei IMEI lookup identifies your device instantly. Premium checks reveal blocklist status, warranty coverage, and Honor sub-brand attribution.

## How to Find Your Huawei IMEI

Four ways to locate the IMEI on any Huawei device:

- **Dial `*#06#`** on the Phone app — both IMEIs and the serial number appear on-screen.
- **Settings → About phone → Status → IMEI** — works on EMUI 10+, HarmonyOS 2.0+, and HarmonyOS NEXT.
- **Original retail box** — Huawei prints the IMEI on the outer barcode sticker alongside the model number (e.g., `ALT-L29`, `PAA-AL10`).
- **SIM tray or rear label** — older models engrave the IMEI on the metal SIM tray or on a sticker beneath the battery cover.

For Huawei MatePad tablets with cellular, the IMEI is in **Settings → About tablet**.

## When Should You Run a Huawei IMEI Check?

- **Buying used in a cross-border marketplace.** A Mate 60 Pro from Shenzhen ships with a Chinese Mainland ROM (no Google, HarmonyOS in Chinese by default). A Mate 60 Pro sold in the UAE has different band support and firmware. The full device report tells you which SKU you're buying.
- **Counterfeit sanity check.** Huawei flagships are among the most-copied Android phones in Southeast Asia. A fake unit will either return no IMEI, a mismatched model, or a TAC that resolves to a completely different brand.
- **Blacklist verification.** Global GSMA blocklisting is aggressively enforced by Huawei-friendly carriers in the MEA and APAC regions.
- **Honor confusion.** Pre-2021 devices (Honor 20 Pro, Honor View 30) sometimes list as Huawei in older databases. Our lookup disambiguates correctly.
- **Warranty transfer.** Huawei's global warranty is honoured across most Huawei service centres, but only for genuine devices with valid IMEI records — verify before paying for a "still under warranty" listing.
- **Import into a new country.** Kirin-powered Mate 60 and Mate 70 devices support Chinese-specific 5G bands. The regional variant field on the full report tells you whether the phone will work on your home carrier.

## What to Do If Your Huawei IMEI Check Reveals a Problem

If the blacklist check flags the device as stolen or lost, treat the transaction as void — the device will not work on GSMA-participating carriers and Huawei will not release the flag without formal proof-of-ownership escalation through a Huawei service centre.

If the model returned by our lookup does not match what the seller claimed (for example, they described a Mate 60 Pro+ but the TAC resolves to a Mate 60 base model), the device has either been misrepresented or, worse, physically swapped internally. Reject the purchase and ask for a refund.

If the regional variant is wrong for your carrier — a Chinese Mainland Mate 70 you were told was the Global SKU — check band compatibility carefully before accepting. Chinese-market devices sometimes drop key sub-6 5G bands used in Europe or North America, and no software update will restore them.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Does an IMEI check work on Huawei phones without Google Services?**
A: Yes. IMEI is a hardware-level identifier assigned at the modem level, completely independent of the operating system or app ecosystem. Our lookup works identically on EMUI, HarmonyOS 2/3/4, and HarmonyOS NEXT.

**Q: Can imeihub tell if my Huawei is a real Huawei or a rebranded Honor?**
A: Yes. Our TAC database is updated to reflect the November 2020 Honor spin-off, so post-2021 Honor devices correctly resolve to Honor. Pre-2021 devices are attributed to Huawei since they were manufactured under the Huawei brand.

**Q: My Mate 60 Pro won't work on my carrier — will an IMEI check help?**
A: It'll tell you the exact regional SKU. A Chinese Mainland Mate 60 Pro supports 5G bands specific to China Mobile, Unicom, and Telecom — most of these overlap with global sub-6 5G but not always. Combined with a carrier lock check, the IMEI report will show whether the issue is regional band mismatch or a network lock.

**Q: Are Huawei counterfeits detectable by IMEI?**
A: In most cases yes. A well-made counterfeit will clone a real Huawei IMEI, but the TAC (first 8 digits) will either not match the claimed model or the paint colour/storage will be inconsistent. Combined with a physical inspection — HarmonyOS boot behaviour, camera app UX, weight — the IMEI check is a strong first filter.

**Q: Can imeihub unlock a Huawei bootloader?**
A: No. Imeihub verifies device status only. Huawei stopped issuing bootloader unlock codes in 2018, and no third party can legitimately unlock a modern Huawei bootloader.

**Q: Does the free check cover Huawei MatePad tablets and Huawei Watch cellular models?**
A: Yes, provided the device has an IMEI (all cellular MatePad and Watch GT LTE variants do). Wi-Fi-only tablets have a serial number but no IMEI.

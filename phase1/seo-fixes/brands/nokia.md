# Nokia IMEI Check — Free Lookup for Nokia Devices

Nokia is one of the most globally recognised mobile brands in history — at its 2007 peak it held over 40% of the world smartphone market. Today the Nokia name lives on under a licensing agreement with HMD Global (Finland), which builds both classic feature phones (2660 Flip, 3210 4G, 8210 4G) and modern Android smartphones (XR21, X30, G60). HMD has also begun selling handsets under its own HMD brand — Skyline, Pulse, Fusion — often on the same TAC allocations. Every Nokia and HMD device ships with a 15-digit IMEI (dual IMEIs on all dual-SIM models) that lets you identify the exact hardware, storage, and regional variant.

Nokia's strongest markets today are India, Vietnam, the Middle East, Africa, and Central and Eastern Europe — regions where feature phones still ship in high volume alongside affordable Android. That makes cross-border used sales common, and it makes IMEI verification particularly important: fake Nokia 3310 clones and counterfeit budget Androids appear regularly in the same regional marketplaces where the genuine article sells.

Our free Nokia IMEI lookup identifies your device instantly. Premium checks reveal blocklist status, warranty coverage, and carrier lock.

## How to Find Your Nokia IMEI

Four methods, all quick:

- **Dial `*#06#`** on the Phone or Dialer app — the IMEI displays immediately. Works identically on Nokia feature phones (S30+, KaiOS) and Nokia Android devices.
- **Settings → About phone → Status → IMEI** on Nokia Android smartphones (Android 11+).
- **Menu → Settings → Phone → Phone information** on feature phones running S30+.
- **Original retail box** — HMD prints the IMEI on the outer barcode label alongside the model number and Type Approval.
- **SIM tray or back cover** — Nokia XR21 and Nokia X30 engrave the IMEI on the SIM tray; feature phones like the 3210 4G have it printed on a sticker beneath the removable back cover.

## When Should You Run a Nokia IMEI Check?

- **Buying a Nokia feature phone as a second phone or backup.** Nokia 3210 4G and 8210 4G reissues are hugely popular. Counterfeits are common — a mismatched IMEI is the fastest tell.
- **Cross-border used purchase in India, MEA, or Southeast Asia.** Nokia and HMD Androids move heavily across borders. Confirm the regional SKU before you pay to avoid band incompatibility.
- **Blacklist verification.** Nokia and HMD devices are covered by GSMA blocklist reporting in most major markets. Always check before paying cash on a marketplace.
- **Warranty check.** HMD's 24-month EU warranty is transferable to a new owner in most cases. Our warranty check confirms remaining coverage before you pay a premium for "still under warranty" claims.
- **HMD-branded confusion.** HMD Skyline and HMD Pulse ship under the HMD brand but share design DNA and TAC ranges with Nokia. Our lookup tells you which brand the specific IMEI is assigned to.
- **Rugged deployment.** Nokia XR21 and XR20 are popular for construction and field-service fleets. Verify the IMEI history and warranty before deploying secondhand units into a fleet.

## What to Do If Your Nokia IMEI Check Reveals a Problem

If the blacklist check flags the device, treat the sale as void — a blocked IMEI will fail to connect on any GSMA-participating carrier across India, the EU, MEA, and most other markets HMD serves. Refunds on cash marketplace deals are notoriously difficult once you've handed over money, so run the blacklist check before payment, not after.

If the model returned differs from what the seller advertised (a Nokia G22 sold as a Nokia XR21, for example), the listing is either mistaken or fraudulent. Ask for a refund and cross-check the physical model number engraved on the SIM tray or beneath the back cover against the IMEI result — the two should match exactly.

If a counterfeit feature phone is suspected — Nokia 3210 4G and 8210 4G clones circulate widely in Asian markets — a "no data found" response from our lookup, combined with a suspiciously light chassis or wrong keypad feel, is a strong signal the device is not a genuine HMD product and should be rejected.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Are HMD Global phones the same as Nokia phones?**
A: Not exactly. HMD Global has held the Nokia phone licence since 2016 and manufactures both Nokia-branded and HMD-branded devices. Nokia-branded phones follow the historic Nokia design language; HMD-branded phones (Skyline, Pulse, Fusion) are HMD's own line. Both share HMD's manufacturing base and warranty programme.

**Q: Does IMEI check work on old Nokia feature phones like the 3310 3G?**
A: Yes, provided the device has a valid IMEI (all 3310 3G, 3310 4G, 3210 4G, 8210 4G, and 2660 Flip models do). Dial `*#06#` or check the sticker beneath the removable back cover.

**Q: Can imeihub unlock my Nokia carrier lock?**
A: No. Imeihub verifies the lock status but doesn't perform unlocks. Carrier unlocking is handled by the original carrier — most Nokia devices unlock automatically after 12 months of paid-off service.

**Q: My Nokia says "insert SIM" even with a valid SIM — is it blacklisted?**
A: Possibly, but check the SIM in another phone first. If it works elsewhere, run a blacklist check on the Nokia's IMEI — a blocklisted device rejects all SIMs on the same GSMA-connected network.

**Q: How do I tell a real Nokia 3210 4G from a fake?**
A: Physical clues (weight, button feel, screen behaviour) plus the IMEI. A genuine 3210 4G has a TAC that resolves to HMD Global manufacturing. Counterfeits typically either return no result or resolve to an unrelated Chinese OEM.

**Q: Does the free IMEI check reveal what version of Android my Nokia shipped with?**
A: The free check shows the shipping OS where the TAC database exposes it (e.g., Nokia G22 shipped with Android 12). The current installed version can only be seen on the device itself under Settings → About.

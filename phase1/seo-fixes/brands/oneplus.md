# OnePlus IMEI Check — Free Lookup for OnePlus Devices

OnePlus launched in 2013 with a single mission: build a flagship-killer that undercut Samsung and Apple on price without compromising on performance. Twelve years and a merger with Oppo (both are BBK Electronics brands) later, OnePlus is a top-ten global smartphone maker with a devoted enthusiast following, the ColorOS-based OxygenOS software, and a two-line portfolio — flagship OnePlus numbered series (13, 12, 11) plus the budget-to-midrange Nord line built for India, Europe, and Southeast Asia. Every OnePlus device ships with a 15-digit IMEI (dual IMEIs on all 4G and 5G units) tied to Oppo's global manufacturing and warranty systems.

OnePlus resale is unusually active for two reasons. First, the community skews toward power users who upgrade frequently, feeding the second-hand market. Second, OnePlus's aggressive India pricing means the same OnePlus 12R can cost 40% less in Bangalore than in the UK — which drives significant grey-market cross-border trade. An IMEI check tells you whether the specific unit is the Indian, Chinese, European, or North American SKU, whether it's carrier-locked, and whether it's been reported stolen.

Our free OnePlus IMEI lookup identifies your model instantly. Premium checks add blocklist status, carrier lock, and warranty coverage.

## How to Find Your OnePlus IMEI

Four reliable methods:

- **Dial `*#06#`** on the Phone app — both IMEIs and the serial number display immediately.
- **Settings → About device → IMEI** on OxygenOS 13, 14, and 15.
- **Original retail box** — OnePlus prints the IMEI on the outer barcode label along with the model number (e.g., `CPH2653` for OnePlus 13) and the Type Approval.
- **SIM tray** — OnePlus flagships engrave the IMEI on the metal SIM tray; some Nord budget models place it on a sticker beneath the rear cover.

For OnePlus Pad 2 with cellular and OnePlus Watch 2 LTE, the IMEI lives in **Settings → About**.

## When Should You Run a OnePlus IMEI Check?

- **Grey-market Indian import.** OnePlus's India pricing is aggressively low, and OnePlus 12R and Nord 4 units regularly appear on European and US marketplaces at grey-import prices. Confirm the model number matches the Indian SKU — Indian units may lack certain 5G bands used in North America.
- **US T-Mobile carrier lock.** T-Mobile is OnePlus's primary US retail carrier, and most T-Mo OnePlus units are locked for 12+ months. Verify status before paying for a "T-Mobile unlocked" listing.
- **Blacklist verification.** OnePlus theft claims tend to concentrate in India and the UK. Always run a blacklist check on a used flagship before paying cash.
- **OxygenOS vs ColorOS confusion.** OnePlus phones sold in China ship with ColorOS (Oppo's skin) rather than OxygenOS. The full report identifies which regional firmware the unit shipped with — worth knowing if you're expecting a specific software experience.
- **Bootloader unlock check.** If you're buying a used OnePlus to flash a custom ROM, verify the current bootloader state. If you're buying one for normal use, verify it hasn't been previously unlocked (which can affect warranty).
- **Counterfeit sanity check.** OnePlus flagships are among the most-cloned Android phones in Southeast Asian markets. A mismatched IMEI is the fastest tell.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What's the difference between an Indian OnePlus 12R and a global OnePlus 12R?**
A: The Indian SKU (CPH2609) supports Indian 5G bands (n1, n3, n5, n7, n8, n28, n40, n41, n77, n78). Global units support additional Western European and Asia-Pacific bands. Our full report identifies the exact model number so you can cross-reference band compatibility with your local carrier.

**Q: Is my OnePlus locked to T-Mobile?**
A: If you bought it from T-Mobile or on a T-Mobile financing plan, almost certainly yes. Run our carrier lock check to confirm. T-Mobile unlocks OnePlus devices automatically after 40 days of active service and full payment on the device, but the unlock is not always applied — verify status before importing internationally.

**Q: Can imeihub tell me if my OnePlus bootloader was previously unlocked?**
A: Where the TAC database exposes it, yes. This matters if you're buying used: an unlocked bootloader means the device could have been flashed with custom firmware, which sometimes invalidates warranty and always resets the security state.

**Q: Does the free IMEI check work on OnePlus Ace models sold only in China?**
A: Yes. OnePlus Ace 3, Ace 3 Pro, and Ace 3V are all standard IMEI devices. The lookup returns the Chinese SKU and confirms the model.

**Q: My OnePlus won't accept a foreign SIM after import — what should I check?**
A: Two things. First, run a carrier lock check — if the device shows locked, that's the cause. Second, cross-reference the exact model number against your target carrier's supported band list — regional SKUs (India, China, Global, North America) support different bands, and a band mismatch can prevent activation even on an unlocked device.

**Q: Can imeihub verify OnePlus Care+ coverage?**

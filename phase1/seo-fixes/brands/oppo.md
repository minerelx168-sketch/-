## How to Find Your OPPO IMEI

Four reliable ways to locate the IMEI on any OPPO device:

**1. Dial `*#06#`.** Every OPPO phone from R7 through Find X8 Pro returns IMEI1, IMEI2, and serial number instantly.

**2. Settings menu (ColorOS 15 / 14).** Go to `Settings > About device > Status > IMEI information`. Older ColorOS 12 or 13 uses `Settings > About phone > Status`.

**3. Original packaging.** OPPO retail boxes include a label with the IMEI, model code (for example `CPH2649` for Find X8 Pro), and colour variant. The colour name printed on the box is the definitive marketing name — check it against the IMEI report.

**4. SIM tray.** Slide out the SIM tray with a SIM ejector. The IMEI is printed on the small metal or plastic surface facing the phone body on most A and Reno series devices.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Are OPPO, realme, vivo, and OnePlus actually the same underneath?**
All four are BBK Electronics brands. They share R&D, factories, hardware platforms, and many component suppliers, but each has an independent brand team, distinct software (ColorOS for OPPO and OnePlus's OxygenOS lineage, OriginOS/FuntouchOS for vivo, realme UI for realme), and different market positioning. Hardware overlap is high; software identity is separate.

**Q: Does an OPPO IMEI check tell me the ColorOS version?**
No — ColorOS version is a software state, not a hardware property. The IMEI report tells you the factory-shipped region, from which the original ColorOS version can be inferred, but the current on-device version comes from `Settings > About device > Version`.

**Q: What does the OPPO model code (for example CPH2649) mean?**
`CPH` is OPPO's global model prefix; the digits identify the SKU. Different regional variants of the same phone use different codes — CPH2649 vs CPH2651, for example, may be the same Find X8 Pro sold in different markets. Our IMEI check returns the marketing name, not the raw code, but you can cross-reference either way.

**Q: Can imeihub remove an OPPO carrier lock or FRP?**
No. imeihub verifies status only. Carrier unlock must be requested through the original carrier; FRP (Factory Reset Protection) requires the previous owner's Google credentials.

**Q: My OPPO won't accept a new SIM — is it blacklisted?**
Possibly, but SIM rejection can also be caused by carrier lock, IMEI blacklisting by a specific carrier, or regional band incompatibility. Run a blacklist premium check and an unlock status check to isolate the cause.

**Q: Do older OPPO F-series and R-series phones support IMEI lookup?**
Yes — as long as the phone has a valid TAC (any OPPO phone sold globally does), our free lookup returns brand, model, and specs. Some very old R-series may return partial spec data if the TAC record is limited.

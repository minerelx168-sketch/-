## How to Find Your realme IMEI

Four ways to find the IMEI on any realme device:

**1. Dial `*#06#`.** Instant IMEI display on every realme phone. Dual-SIM models show IMEI1 and IMEI2.

**2. Settings (realme UI 6.0 / 5.0).** `Settings > About device > Status > IMEI information`. Older realme UI 3 or 4: `Settings > About phone > Status`.

**3. Original box.** realme retail packaging shows IMEI, model code (for example `RMX3841` for GT7 Pro), and colour variant on the outside label. Narzo box designs differ visually but carry the same label format.

**4. SIM tray.** Most realme phones print the IMEI on the plastic SIM tray or on a small label under the removable back on older C-series models.

## When Should You Run a realme IMEI Check?

Common realme IMEI check scenarios:

- **Used realme purchase.** Verify claimed model, storage, colour, and region match before paying. The used realme market in India, Indonesia, Bangladesh, and the Philippines is enormous and mixed.
- **Grey-market import verification.** realme SKUs vary significantly by region; a phone made for India may lack bands used in Egypt or Nigeria, and warranty in one market doesn't transfer to another.
- **Narzo and C-series counterfeit check.** Fake Narzo and C series devices appear in offline markets across South and Southeast Asia. TAC verification is the fastest way to catch them.
- **Warranty verification.** realme warranty is country-specific; a realme sold in Indonesia is generally not covered in Malaysia or Thailand.
- **Blacklist check.** Stolen realme devices are resold cross-border; GSMA verification catches most that were reported to a participating carrier.

Two regional notes worth keeping in mind. Second, realme's Indian and Indonesian SKUs frequently ship with region-locked pricing on realme Store promotions; a phone bought at "India price" on the used market may report an India TAC even if the seller claims it as Global, which affects warranty eligibility if you later travel or move.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is realme really a separate brand from OPPO?**
realme was spun off from OPPO in 2018 as an independent brand under BBK Electronics. It has its own product team, its own supply chain agreements, its own firmware (realme UI, evolved from ColorOS), and its own marketing. Hardware platforms overlap with OPPO in some tiers, but each brand ships distinct SKUs and each is separately supported by our IMEI database.

**Q: Are Narzo and realme the same?**
Narzo is realme's value-performance sub-brand, aimed primarily at India, Indonesia, and other price-sensitive markets. Narzo devices run realme UI, are supported by realme service centres, and are covered by our free and premium IMEI checks. The IMEI report identifies each Narzo model by its full marketing name.

**Q: How can I tell if my realme is a Global or India variant?**
Model codes and IMEI TACs differ per region. Our free lookup returns the marketing name; a premium info report includes the sales region and warranty country explicitly. If the box says "India" but the IMEI reports Global, the phone was likely imported grey-market.

**Q: Can imeihub remove a realme Account lock or FRP?**
No. imeihub is a verification service. realme Account and FRP locks can only be released by the previous owner or by realme support with proof of ownership.

**Q: My realme GT is showing "Enter your realme account" after factory reset — what does that mean?**
The device is bound to a previous owner's realme Account (activation lock). Contact the seller and ask them to sign out from their realme Cloud, or return the phone if they cannot. A premium check before purchase catches this before the money changes hands.

**Q: Does an IMEI check work on realme Pad tablets?**
Only on cellular (LTE) Pad models. Wi-Fi-only Pad tablets do not have an IMEI, so IMEI databases cannot identify them.

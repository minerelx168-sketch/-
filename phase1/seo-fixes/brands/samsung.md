## How to Find Your Samsung IMEI

You have four reliable ways to locate the 15-digit IMEI on any Galaxy device:

**1. Dial `*#06#`.** Open the Phone app dialer and type `*#06#`. The IMEI appears instantly. Dual-SIM Galaxy models show IMEI1 and IMEI2. This works on every Galaxy phone from the Galaxy S2 to the S24 Ultra, including foldables.

**2. Settings menu.** Go to `Settings > About phone > Status information > IMEI information`. On Galaxy Tab tablets the path is `Settings > About tablet > Status`. One UI 6 and 7 use the same navigation. Long-press the IMEI to copy it.

**3. Original packaging.** Every Samsung retail box has a white label on the side or bottom showing the IMEI, model number (for example `SM-S928B/DS`), and serial number. The `/DS` suffix means dual-SIM; `/N` is a Korean market variant; `/U` is US carrier.

**4. SIM tray or rear label.** On most Galaxy A and older S series phones, the SIM tray or the label under the removable back cover lists the IMEI. Newer flagships with sealed backs may only show it via `*#06#`.

If the phone will not power on, use the box or the purchase receipt. Do not trust an IMEI written on a sticker inside a used-market box unless you also verify it against `*#06#` on the powered-on device.

Several scenarios call for a Samsung IMEI check before you pay or activate:

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Is Samsung Knox Guard the same as a blacklist?**
No. Knox Guard is Samsung's enterprise MDM programme — it lets a company enforce policies on devices it deploys, and those policies survive factory reset. A blacklist entry is a separate flag from a mobile carrier (usually reported through the GSMA database) indicating the device was reported lost or stolen. A Samsung device can be Knox-locked but not blacklisted, blacklisted but not Knox-enrolled, or both.

**Q: Will an IMEI check tell me if my Galaxy is a US or international model?**
Yes. The TAC (Type Allocation Code — first 8 digits of the IMEI) is assigned per regional SKU. Our free Samsung check returns the marketing model and, where the TAC distinguishes it, the regional variant. Model number confirmation via `Settings > About phone > Model number` is the definitive check: `SM-S928U` is US, `SM-S928B` is EMEA/APAC, `SM-S9280` is China.

**Q: Can imeihub remove Knox Guard from my Galaxy?**
No. imeihub is a verification service. Knox Guard can only be released by the enrolling enterprise's Samsung Knox administrator. We can tell you the Knox status, but we cannot change it.

**Q: My phone shows two IMEIs — which one do I check?**
Enter IMEI1 (the primary). Both IMEIs belong to the same physical device and return the same model information.

**Q: Does a Samsung IMEI check work on Galaxy tablets?**
Only tablets with a cellular modem (LTE or 5G models) have an IMEI. Wi-Fi-only Galaxy Tabs use a serial number instead, which is not supported by IMEI databases.

**Q: How current is the model database?**
Our TAC database covers Galaxy models released through 2025 and is refreshed regularly as new SKUs are registered.

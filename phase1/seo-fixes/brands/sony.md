## How to Find Your Sony Xperia IMEI

Four reliable ways to find the IMEI on any Sony device:

**1. Dial `*#06#`.** Works on every Xperia from the Xperia Z1 through Xperia 1 VI. Returns IMEI1, IMEI2 (dual-SIM), and serial.

**2. Settings menu (Android 14+ on Xperia).** Go to `Settings > About phone > Status information > IMEI (SIM slot 1)`. Older Xperia Android 12 or 13 uses `Settings > About phone > Status`.

**3. Original packaging.** Sony Xperia boxes have a white label on the side listing the IMEI, model number (for example `XQ-EC54` for Xperia 1 VI), and region code.

**4. SIM tray.** Slide the SIM tray out with a SIM ejector. The IMEI is printed on the plastic tray or on a small label inside the tray slot on newer Xperia flagships.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What's the difference between XQ-series and SO-series Xperia?**
`XQ-` prefixes are Sony's global unlocked models — sold via Sony's own channels in Europe, MENA, and Southeast Asia. `SO-` prefixes are Japan-only models sold through SoftBank, Docomo, or au (KDDI). They share hardware but differ in band support, carrier lock status, and warranty region. Model number is printed on the retail box and confirmed by the IMEI report.

**Q: Are Japanese Xperia phones unlocked?**
Not by default. Older Japan-carrier Xperias were often carrier-locked; newer ones (2021 and later) can typically be unlocked by the original carrier after certain conditions are met, but the unlock is not automatic. Always run a network unlock status check before buying an imported Japanese Xperia.

**Q: Does the IMEI check reveal the exact Xperia model number?**
Yes — the marketing name (for example "Xperia 1 VI") is returned by our free check. The exact model number (XQ-EC54, XQ-EC72, SO-51E, and so on) can also be confirmed via a premium full info report.

**Q: Can imeihub unlock a SoftBank or Docomo Xperia?**
No. imeihub verifies status only. Carrier unlock must be requested through the original Japanese carrier, subject to their eligibility policies.

**Q: My Xperia is showing "SIM not supported" — is it blacklisted?**
Possibly, but SIM rejection can also be caused by carrier lock (very common for Japanese-market Xperia) or band incompatibility. Run a blacklist check and a network unlock status check to isolate the cause.

**Q: Are Xperia tablets supported?**
Sony no longer manufactures new Xperia Tablet models, but any legacy cellular Xperia Tablet with a valid TAC returns full IMEI data. Wi-Fi-only Xperia Tablets have no IMEI and are not identifiable via TAC databases.

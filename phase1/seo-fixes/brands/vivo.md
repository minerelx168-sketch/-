# vivo IMEI Check — Free Lookup for vivo Devices

vivo is one of the world's top-five smartphone makers and part of the BBK Electronics family alongside OPPO, realme, OnePlus, and iQOO. The company ships over 90 million phones per year, with dominant positions in China and India and rapidly growing shares across Southeast Asia, MENA, and Latin America. vivo's product line spans the X series (flagship photography phones with ZEISS optics), the V series (mid-premium camera-focused devices), the Y series (mainstream and entry-level), and the iQOO gaming and performance sub-brand.

vivo's regional segmentation is aggressive. ## How to Find Your vivo IMEI

Four ways to find the IMEI on any vivo device:

**1. Dial `*#06#`.** Works on every vivo phone. Returns IMEI1, IMEI2 (dual-SIM), and serial.

**2. Settings (FuntouchOS 15 / OriginOS).** `Settings > About phone > Status > IMEI information`. On older FuntouchOS 12 or 13: `Settings > More settings > About phone > Status`.

**3. Original packaging.** vivo retail boxes have a label showing the IMEI, model code (for example `V2338` for X200 Pro), and colour variant. iQOO packaging uses its own black-and-orange design but the label format is identical.

**4. SIM tray.** vivo prints the IMEI on the plastic SIM tray on most Y and V series phones. Slide the tray out and look at the flat inner surface.

## When Should You Run a vivo IMEI Check?

Situations that warrant a vivo IMEI check:

- **Used vivo purchase.** Verify claimed model, storage, colour, and region match the physical phone before you pay. This is the single most common use case in South Asia and SEA used markets.
- **CN OriginOS vs Global FuntouchOS check.** vivo's Chinese firmware ships without Google Mobile Services. If you need Play Store and Google apps, you need a Global SKU — the IMEI report tells you which you're actually looking at.
- **Y-series counterfeit check.** Fakes of popular Y100 and Y03 models are common in offline markets across South Asia and SEA. TAC verification catches almost all of them instantly.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Are vivo and iQOO the same brand?**
iQOO is vivo's performance and gaming sub-brand under the wider BBK Electronics group. It uses vivo's factories and shares hardware platforms but has its own marketing team and firmware branding (iQOO Monster UI branding on top of OriginOS/FuntouchOS). Our IMEI check identifies each correctly.

**Q: What's the difference between FuntouchOS and OriginOS?**
FuntouchOS is vivo's global firmware — Google services, English and regional language packs, standard Play Store. OriginOS is the Chinese-market firmware — Chinese app stores, no Google Mobile Services out of the box. Both are Android-based, but the app ecosystems are different enough that you cannot easily use one in the other's market.

**Q: Does an IMEI check reveal the vivo model code (like V2338)?**
The report returns the marketing name (for example "vivo X200 Pro"). Model codes are internal SKU identifiers; you can cross-reference them on the phone via `Settings > About phone > Model`, or on the retail box label.

**Q: Can imeihub unlock a carrier-locked vivo?**
No. imeihub verifies status only. Carrier unlock must be requested through the original carrier, subject to their eligibility policies.

**Q: My vivo Y series is asking for a "Cloud" login after factory reset — is that FRP?**
Possibly. vivo Cloud login prompts can indicate Factory Reset Protection binding to the previous owner's vivo Account. Only the previous owner can release it; verify status with a premium check before purchase and return the phone if the seller cannot sign out.

**Q: Are older vivo Y-series and V-series supported?**
Yes — any vivo phone with a valid TAC returns full free-check data. Older models (Y11, V11) may have less granular spec data but core model identification is reliable.

# Xiaomi IMEI Check — Free Lookup for Xiaomi, Redmi & POCO Devices

Xiaomi is the world's third-largest smartphone maker behind Samsung and Apple, and it operates one of the most diverse portfolios in the industry — flagship Xiaomi numbered series, the Redmi budget and mid-tier lineup, POCO performance devices, and the Xiaomi 15 Ultra premium tier. Combined shipments across the three brands exceeded 168 million units in 2024, and the company continues to lead in emerging markets across South Asia, Southeast Asia, MENA, and Africa.

That scale creates two problems for buyers. First, Xiaomi runs distinct regional ROMs: China ROM (MIUI China or HyperOS China, pre-installed with Chinese app stores and lacking Google services), Global ROM (Play Store, English and regional language packs), EEA ROM (EU-compliant), and India ROM (regional apps). A phone bought in Shenzhen ships with the wrong ROM for a user in Manila or Dubai, and grey-market importers routinely reflash Global ROM onto China SKUs — sometimes badly. Second, Xiaomi hardware is one of the most heavily counterfeited in the world: fake Redmi Note and POCO devices flood offline markets in Dhaka, Karachi, Manila, Lagos, and Cairo.

An imeihub Xiaomi IMEI check takes three seconds to reveal the true model, storage, colour, and release year. If the phone in your hand is labelled Redmi Note 13 Pro but the IMEI reports a Redmi Note 11, you have a counterfeit or a rebadged device. Premium Xiaomi checks add Mi Account lock status (Mi Cloud), full Xiaomi info reports, and blacklist verification — essential before importing a Xiaomi device to a new region or buying used.

## How to Find Your Xiaomi IMEI

Four ways to locate the IMEI on Xiaomi, Redmi, and POCO phones:

**1. Dial `*#06#`.** Works on every Xiaomi, Redmi, and POCO device. The IMEI screen displays IMEI1, IMEI2 (dual-SIM), MEID, and serial number.

**2. Settings menu (HyperOS 2 / MIUI 14).** Go to `Settings > About phone > All specs > Status`, then scroll to IMEI. On older MIUI 12 or 13 the path is `Settings > About phone > Status > IMEI`.

**3. Original box.** Xiaomi retail boxes have a barcode label on the bottom listing the IMEI, model code (for example `2405CRPFDG` for a Xiaomi 14T), and the regional variant (CN, GLOBAL, EEA, IN).

**4. SIM tray.** Most Redmi and POCO phones print the IMEI on the plastic SIM tray. Slide the tray out with the SIM ejector tool and look at the flat surface.

## When Should You Run a Xiaomi IMEI Check?

Xiaomi IMEI verification is especially important in these scenarios:

- **Buying used in a grey-market channel.** The Xiaomi used market in South Asia, Southeast Asia, and Africa is huge. A three-second IMEI check catches mismatched models, reflashed China ROMs, and outright counterfeits before you pay.
- **Verifying a Global vs China ROM claim.** If the box says GLOBAL but the IMEI report says CN, the phone was manufactured for the China market and reflashed. That may work for you or it may not, but you deserve to know before you buy.
- **Counterfeit check.** Fake Redmi and POCO devices are common in offline markets. The IMEI TAC reveals the actual manufacturer and model. If a "Redmi Note 13 Pro" reports as an unknown MediaTek reference device, walk away.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: How can I check if my Xiaomi is a genuine Global ROM vs a rebadged China ROM?**
The IMEI TAC (first 8 digits) reveals the original manufacturer variant assigned at production. If the TAC reports a CN-region model but the current MIUI or HyperOS shows Global settings and Play Store, the device was reflashed after manufacture. A premium Xiaomi info report makes this explicit and includes the factory firmware region.

**Q: What is a Mi Account lock and how is it different from a screen lock?**
Mi Account lock (part of Mi Cloud's Find Device feature) ties the phone to the original owner's Xiaomi ID. It survives factory reset, hardware reflash, and even motherboard swap in some cases. A screen lock (PIN, pattern, fingerprint) is a local device credential; Mi Account lock is a cloud-enforced ownership binding.

**Q: Can imeihub remove a Mi Account lock?**
No. imeihub is a verification service. Only the original Mi Account holder — or Xiaomi support with proof of purchase — can remove a Mi Account lock.

**Q: Are POCO phones the same as Xiaomi or Redmi?**
POCO is a Xiaomi sub-brand. POCO devices share Xiaomi hardware platforms and firmware base but market to a different segment (gaming, spec-for-price). Our IMEI check returns the exact POCO model name and covers all POCO devices.

**Q: My Redmi is showing "Not authenticated" — is that a Mi Account lock?**
It may be. "Not authenticated" usually means Mi Cloud has locked the device to another Xiaomi ID. Run a Mi Account premium check to confirm.

**Q: Does an IMEI check work on Xiaomi tablets and Redmi Pads?**
Only on cellular (LTE or 5G) versions. Wi-Fi-only Pad tablets do not have an IMEI, so IMEI databases cannot identify them.

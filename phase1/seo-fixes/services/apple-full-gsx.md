# Apple Full GSX Report — Complete iPhone Service, Hardware & AppleCare History

The Apple Full GSX Report is the deepest, most authoritative iPhone verification available outside an Apple Store service counter. It returns everything Apple knows about the device tied to a given IMEI — the exact hardware configuration as manufactured, every recorded service event, whether the unit has ever been replaced or reworked, the AppleCare enrollment status, and the full warranty timeline. If you are transacting on a $800+ used iPhone, buying a bulk lot for resale, or building a forensic chain of custody for insurance or legal purposes, this is the report that removes doubt.

Compared with the entry-level Apple Basic Info check, the Full GSX Report is a superset. Basic Info tells you the model and warranty; the GSX report tells you whether that iPhone 15 Pro Max was originally a 256GB or 512GB, whether the screen has been replaced under warranty, whether the battery was swapped in 2024, whether the logic board was ever reworked, and whether the unit is one of the flagged replacement swaps Apple issued after a service escalation. In the used-iPhone market — especially in Bangladesh, Egypt, Nigeria, and Southeast Asia — units are routinely misgraded, and only a GSX-level read will surface the truth.

The premium price of $4.20 reflects both the tier of data returned and the certified-partner cost of authorised GSX access. Casual buyers rarely need this level of depth; high-value transactions almost always do. Use it when the cost of getting the purchase wrong exceeds the cost of the report by two or more orders of magnitude.

## What This Check Reveals

A Full GSX Report includes, at a minimum:

- **Full model identity** — SKU, storage, color, part number, sales region.
- **Serial number, IMEI, IMEI2** (for dual-SIM units), MEID, ICCID as recorded at manufacture.
- **Hardware configuration** — original memory, storage tier, cellular technology, and any factory-installed feature flags.
- **Activation status and initial activation date** — where and when the device first came online.
- **Warranty coverage** — limited warranty status, expiration date, purchase date, and country of coverage.
- **AppleCare and AppleCare+ enrollment** — plan type, start date, expiration date, remaining incidents where applicable.
- **Service and repair history** — every event Apple has logged: screen repairs, battery replacements, board-level repairs, whole-unit replacements, and the service provider that handled the work.
- **Replacement / rework flags** — whether this specific IMEI is a factory refurbished swap, a service-issued replacement, or an original retail unit.
- **Loaner status** — whether the device is currently registered as a loaner in Apple's system (rare but decisive).
- **Find My status hints** and MDM enrollment indicators where available in the tier of data returned.
- **Country of purchase and sales channel** — retail, education, business, telecom carrier bundle, and so on.

Because the report is comprehensive, it also serves as excellent evidence documentation — a PDF-exportable snapshot of the state of the device on the day of purchase.

## How It Works

You submit the IMEI (or serial number) via your imeihub dashboard, choose Apple Full GSX Report, and the request is routed to certified IMEI API partners with authorised access to the GSX-tier data pipeline. Apple's own Global Service Exchange system underpins the response; imeihub is not scraping any consumer page. Delivery is typically under a minute, with a five-minute service level for edge cases when Apple's backend is slow.

You receive a rich, structured report in your dashboard, downloadable and shareable via a permanent link. Reports are archived to your account indefinitely so you can reference them at resale time, during warranty claims, or when disputing a marketplace transaction.

## Common Use Cases

**High-value used iPhone purchase ($800+).** Someone is selling an iPhone 15 Pro Max 1TB for $1,050. That saving over new is only real if the phone is genuinely a 1TB unit, has never had a logic-board repair, still has warranty or AppleCare, and has not been swapped as a service replacement (which often changes resale value). A $4.20 report protects a four-figure transaction.

**Business bulk verification.** A reseller is buying 50 used iPhones from a wholesaler. Full GSX on each unit — total investment $210 — surfaces the two or three units that were misgraded, the one that is a service replacement flagged as "do not resell", and any that carry hidden repair history. The reseller either negotiates the wholesaler down or refuses those units. A single flagged unit typically returns the cost of checking the whole batch.

**Forensic and legal documentation.** Insurance adjusters, corporate IT audit teams, and used-device dealers use Full GSX reports as evidence in disputes, chain-of-custody records, and internal fraud investigations. A timestamped report from an authorised pipeline carries more weight than a screenshot from a free site.

**Pre-shipment quality control for cross-border trade.** Importers moving iPhones between the US, EU, Middle East, and Africa run Full GSX to confirm the sales region, activation state, and repair history match the trade documents. A single mismatch caught early avoids customs, warranty, or buyer disputes later.

**Pre-AppleCare decision.** If you have just bought a used iPhone and are deciding whether to enroll in AppleCare+ (the 60-day window is tight), Full GSX confirms whether the device is even eligible — units with unauthorised repairs or certain replacement flags are not.

## What to Do If Results Are Negative

- **Service history shows repairs the seller did not disclose** — negotiate down or refuse. Undisclosed board-level repairs in particular are a resale-value killer.
- **Unit flagged as service replacement or refurbished** — not necessarily bad (Apple's replacements are high quality), but the resale price should reflect it. Do not pay original-unit prices.
- **AppleCare shows expired or never enrolled** when the seller claimed otherwise — the seller is either mistaken or dishonest.
- **Warranty already expired plus multiple repairs on record** — this device has been through the wars; treat the purchase as parts-value only unless you love the deal.
- **Model configuration differs from what is on the box** — the box has been swapped or the phone has been substituted. Walk away.
- **"Do not resell" or loaner flag present** — do not proceed; the device belongs to a service provider or is otherwise not legally re-sellable.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Why is the Full GSX Report so much more expensive than Basic Info?**
A: The GSX tier data is more tightly controlled by Apple, requires certified service provider access through authorised partners, and returns roughly ten times as many fields. The higher per-check cost also reflects the value it protects — a single accurate GSX report typically saves the buyer hundreds of dollars on a bad transaction.

**Q: Does the report show which Apple Store or service centre performed each repair?**
A: In most cases yes — the service provider type (Apple Store, Apple Authorised Service Provider, or Apple-Certified Independent Repair Provider) is included, and often the specific location. In some jurisdictions and for older records, only the type is shown.

**Q: Will running a GSX report void my warranty or affect my AppleCare plan?**
A: No. It is a read-only query against Apple's records. Nothing about the device or its warranty state changes.

**Q: The report says "Repair record" but the previous owner swears the phone has never been serviced. Who is right?**
A: The GSX record. If Apple logged a repair, a repair happened — either the previous owner forgot, was not the one who took it in, or purchased the device after the repair without being told. Trust the report.

**Q: Can Full GSX show me if the phone is iCloud-locked?**
A: Full GSX includes activation status and, in most tiers, Find My status indicators. For a dedicated Clean/Lost verdict, run the [Apple iCloud check](/service.php?slug=apple-icloud) — it is optimised for that single question and much cheaper if that is all you need.

**Q: I bought a "brand new sealed" iPhone but the GSX report shows a purchase date two years ago. What happened?**
A: The unit is not new. It was sold two years ago, then re-boxed and re-shrink-wrapped for resale — a common grey-market tactic. Refuse the transaction or renegotiate to used-condition pricing.

**Q: Is the report accepted as evidence for insurance or marketplace disputes?**
A: It is widely accepted because the data originates from Apple's own GSX system through authorised commercial pipelines. Save the permanent report link and download the PDF for your records.

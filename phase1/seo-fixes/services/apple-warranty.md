# Apple Warranty Check — Confirm Coverage, Expiration & AppleCare by IMEI

The Apple Warranty check by IMEI is the fastest way to answer the question every used iPhone buyer, insurance claimant, resale valuator, and AppleCare shopper eventually asks: how much warranty is left on this device? For approximately $0.10 per check (see the service page for current pricing), the report returns whether the iPhone is currently under Apple's standard limited warranty, whether it carries an active AppleCare or AppleCare+ plan, when coverage expires, and the country in which the coverage was originally issued.

That single set of facts feeds many decisions. A used iPhone with six months of AppleCare+ remaining is worth meaningfully more than an identical unit that is out of warranty. An insurance claim on a damaged iPhone is far easier when you can prove coverage state on the day of the incident. A newly purchased used iPhone that is still within its 60-day AppleCare+ eligibility window can be enrolled to add up to three years of accident protection — but only if you check first. A 10-cent lookup gives you the facts needed to act on any of these.

This service is a focused subset of the Apple portfolio. It costs less than the full [Apple Basic Info check](/service.php?slug=apple-basic) because it returns fewer fields (no activation status, no telephone technology, no model detail) and less than the [Full GSX report](/service.php?slug=apple-full-gsx) because it does not include hardware config or service history. If warranty status is your only question, this is your service.

## What This Check Reveals

The report returns the current Apple coverage picture for the IMEI:

- **Limited warranty status** — active or expired. Apple's standard one-year hardware warranty runs from the initial activation date; the check confirms whether that window is still open.
- **Warranty expiration date** — the specific date the standard warranty ends (or ended).
- **AppleCare / AppleCare+ status** — whether an AppleCare or AppleCare+ plan is currently active on this IMEI, the plan type, and its expiration date. AppleCare+ extends hardware coverage and adds accident protection; AppleCare (older tier) extends hardware coverage only.
- **Purchase country / country of coverage** — the country whose warranty terms apply. Warranty is generally honoured worldwide by Apple for AppleCare+ plans but the country of purchase affects walk-in Apple Store service eligibility, especially for older non-AppleCare units.
- **Purchase / activation date (when returned)** — helpful for verifying age-of-device claims and for AppleCare+ eligibility calculations (the 60-day post-purchase window for adding AppleCare+ starts from purchase date, not check date).

The report does **not** include repair history, service events, or hardware configuration. For those, use the [Apple Full GSX report](/service.php?slug=apple-full-gsx).

## Common Use Cases

**Second-hand iPhone valuation.** You are buying or selling a used iPhone. Warranty and AppleCare state directly affect fair market price. A 10-cent check settles disputes — no more "I think it's still under AppleCare, I bought it in 2023 or 2024?". Show the report, agree on a price, close the deal.

**Insurance claim documentation.** Filing an accidental damage or theft claim with a third-party phone insurer? Insurers routinely ask for proof of warranty state on the day of the incident. A timestamped imeihub report is exactly that.

**AppleCare+ purchase decision.** You just bought a used iPhone. AppleCare+ can still be added within 60 days of the original purchase (in most regions), which typically requires taking the device into an Apple Store or booking a phone diagnostic. Before you invest that time, check whether the device is within the eligibility window and whether AppleCare+ is already active on it (some sellers do not know).

**Refurbisher and reseller inventory audit.** Refurbishers running fleets of hundreds of used iPhones use warranty checks to segment stock: units with active AppleCare+ into a premium resale tier, units in standard warranty into a mid-tier, and out-of-warranty units into a value tier. Pricing by warranty state increases blended margin.

**Cross-border warranty planning.** Importing an iPhone from a different country? The country-of-coverage field tells you whether local Apple walk-in service will honour warranty repairs. AppleCare+ is generally more portable than standard warranty; standard warranty is technically limited to the country of purchase for many models.

## What to Do If Results Are Negative

- **Out of warranty and no AppleCare.** Factor the loss of coverage into your purchase or resale price. If you are the new owner and the device is within 60 days of original purchase, you may still be able to add AppleCare+ — check with Apple Support or an Apple Store immediately, because the window closes quickly.
- **AppleCare recently expired.** AppleCare cannot generally be reinstated once expired. If the device is out of warranty and out of AppleCare, budget for out-of-warranty Apple service pricing (which is significant for high-end iPhones).
- **Warranty active but AppleCare expired.** Standard warranty covers hardware defects only, no accidental damage. If accident protection matters to you (it usually should for iPhones), consider third-party phone insurance for the remaining year of ownership.
- **Country of coverage is different from your country.** For standard warranty repairs, Apple may direct you back to the country of purchase. AppleCare+ is typically portable. Factor this into resale price for cross-border sales.
- **AppleCare status contradicts the seller's claim.** Trust the report over the seller's memory. If the seller claims active AppleCare and the report shows expired, either the seller is mistaken about when they bought AppleCare or (rarely) confused this device with another they own.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Can I add AppleCare+ based on this check's result?**
A: The check tells you whether AppleCare+ is currently active and, indirectly, whether you might still be within the enrollment window. To actually add AppleCare+, you need to go through Apple (in an Apple Store, via the Apple Support app, or on Apple's website) with the device physically available for a diagnostic. imeihub does not sell AppleCare — it verifies status only.

**Q: Is the warranty expiration date the same worldwide?**
A: The standard limited warranty period is 12 months from initial activation in almost all regions. Statutory consumer protection in some countries (notably EU member states, Australia, and parts of Latin America) extends effective coverage beyond Apple's stated warranty for certain classes of defect — those extensions are legal rights and are not reflected in Apple's warranty date field. Consult local consumer protection law if a device fails outside Apple's warranty.

**Q: The check says "Warranty active" but the phone is more than a year old. How is that possible?**
A: Two common reasons. First, the initial activation happened later than the retail purchase (common if the phone sat on a shelf or was given as a gift months after purchase). Second, an active AppleCare or AppleCare+ plan extends the coverage window well beyond the standard one year.

**Q: My warranty expired last week. Can Apple reinstate it?**
A: Almost never for the standard warranty. If you are still within 60 days of original purchase, you may still be able to add AppleCare+ retroactively (which is not the same as reinstating the standard warranty but usually gives you more comprehensive coverage). Contact Apple Support to check.

**Q: Does this check show warranty issued by the retailer (e.g., Best Buy, Costco) instead of Apple?**
A: No. This check only shows warranty and AppleCare state as recorded in Apple's own system. Third-party retailer warranties (SquareTrade, Asurion, Costco's extended warranty) are separate and must be verified with the retailer directly.

**Q: Can I trust the country of coverage for cross-border warranty claims?**
A: Yes for the informational value — it is Apple's record of where the device was sold. Whether Apple in your country will honour a repair on a device from a different country depends on the specific model, the specific issue, and Apple's regional policy. For AppleCare+, coverage is typically portable; for standard warranty, expect regional restrictions.

About this check

The fastest way to identify any mobile phone. Enter the 15-digit IMEI, and we return the manufacturer, model, model number and basic specifications. Works on iPhone, Samsung, Huawei, Xiaomi, and any GSM phone in the world.

How to find your IMEI

  • Dial *#06# on the phone — the IMEI shows up immediately.
  • Or open Settings → About phone → IMEI.
  • Or check the printed label inside the SIM tray / on the box.
